Every student should have to learn a second language. It is important. There are many reasons.

First, it helps you get a job. Second, you can travel. Third, it is good for your brain. My cousin speaks Spanish and English and she got a job at a hospital because she could talk to more patients.

Some people think it takes too much time away from other subjects like math and science. But you can learn a language a little bit every day and it adds up.

That is why learning a second language should be required.
