Phones should be allowed at lunch but not in class. Phones are a big distraction. In class people text and watch videos instead of listening to the teacher. I have seen this happen in almost every class I have.

But at lunch it is different. Lunch is our free time and some students need to call their parents or check the bus schedule. Banning phones all day is to strict and students will just hide them anyway.

So the best rule is phones away in class and allowed at lunch. This way students can focus when they need to and still have freedom.
