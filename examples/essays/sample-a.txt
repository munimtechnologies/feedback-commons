School should start later in the morning. Many teenagers stay up late because their bodies are not ready to sleep at nine or ten. When the first bell rings at 7:30, a lot of students are still tired and they can't pay attention in first period.

My friend falls asleep in math almost every day. If school started at 8:30, he would get one more hour of sleep. Some people say that buses and sports practice would be harder to plan. That is true, but schools could move practice a little later too.

In conclusion, starting later is a good idea because students would learn more.
